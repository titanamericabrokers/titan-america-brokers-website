Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This policy explains what Titan America Brokers collects through this website, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

We collect only what you send us. Through our quote, dealer, and contact forms that includes your name, dealership or company name, phone number, email address, pickup and delivery locations, vehicle details, and any notes you add. If you call or email us, we keep the details you provide in that conversation.

How we use it

  • To price your transport request and respond with a quote
  • To arrange and coordinate the transport of your vehicle or freight
  • To contact you by phone, email, or text about your request and any active shipment
  • To follow up about future transport needs

Who we share it with

To arrange transport we share the details a carrier needs to complete the move — pickup and delivery address, contact name and phone number, and vehicle information — with the motor carrier assigned to your shipment. We may also share information with service providers that help us operate this website or process form submissions, and where required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number, we may text you about your quote or an active shipment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated to say so.

Data retention

We keep request and shipment records for as long as needed to service the account, meet our record-keeping obligations, and resolve any disputes.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information you've given us, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@titanamericabrokers.com or calling (800) 818-6927. California residents may have additional rights regarding personal information under state law.

Children

This website is intended for business and adult consumer use. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top reflects the most recent version.
