Rates can change if the vehicle details, condition, locations, timing, or accessibility differ from what was provided, or if carrier market conditions change before the load is assigned.

Scheduling and transit times

Pickup and delivery windows are estimates. Weather, traffic, mechanical issues, road closures, and carrier scheduling can move them. We will communicate changes as soon as we know about them, but we do not guarantee specific pickup or delivery dates or times.

Vehicle condition and cargo claims

A condition report should be completed and signed at both pickup and delivery. Inspect the vehicle before signing the delivery report and note any new damage on it. Cargo liability for loss or damage in transit rests with the motor carrier that performed the transport, under its own insurance and applicable federal law. We will assist with the claims process and provide carrier documentation, but we are not the insurer of the shipment.

Your responsibilities

  • Remove personal belongings and loose items unless otherwise agreed in writing
