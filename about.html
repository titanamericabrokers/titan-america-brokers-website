About

A Los Angeles Brokerage, Coordinating Nationwide

We are a transport broker. We do not own trucks. Our job is to source qualified carriers, price the lane honestly, and keep you informed until the vehicle is delivered.

What a broker actually does

Carriers own the trucks and haul the freight. A broker connects the vehicle to the right truck, verifies that truck is legal and insured, negotiates the rate, and manages the move from dispatch to delivery.

That means when a lane is tight, you have someone working the phones instead of one truck's schedule. And when something goes sideways at pickup, you have one number to call.
